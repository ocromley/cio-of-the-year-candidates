About the Award

The SIM Chicago CIO of the Year award recognizes exceptional technology leadership

CIO of the Year Award

The SIM Chicago CIO of the Year award is one of the most prestigious honors in the Chicago technology community. It recognizes Chief Information Officers who have demonstrated exceptional leadership, innovation, and impact in their organizations and the broader technology ecosystem.

Selection Criteria:

  • Demonstrated leadership in driving technology strategy and digital transformation
  • Proven ability to deliver business value through innovative technology solutions
  • Commitment to developing talent and building high-performing teams
  • Contributions to the technology community through mentorship and thought leadership
  • Excellence in aligning IT initiatives with organizational goals and objectives

About the Event:

The "Meet the Candidates" event provides a unique opportunity for the SIM Chicago community to engage directly with the nominated CIOs. Attendees will hear firsthand about each candidate's vision, accomplishments, and approach to technology leadership. This intimate gathering fosters meaningful connections among Chicago's top technology executives and celebrates the outstanding contributions of our finalists.

About SIM Chicago

Society for Information Management

SIM is the premier network for IT leaders, comprised of over 5,000 members across 40 chapters in the United States. For over five decades, SIM has been the leading organization for senior IT executives, providing a unique forum for networking, professional development, and thought leadership.

Our Mission:

  • To nurture a community of senior IT executives who support each other in their professional growth
  • To foster meaningful connections between IT leaders across industries
  • To advance the IT profession through knowledge sharing and mentorship
  • To promote diversity and inclusion in technology leadership

SIM Chicago Chapter:

The Chicago chapter is one of SIM's most active communities, hosting regular events that bring together CIOs, CTOs, and senior technology leaders from the region's top organizations. Our CIO of the Year program celebrates the exceptional leaders who are driving innovation and excellence in technology across Chicago.
